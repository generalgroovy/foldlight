Project & keyboard help

Keyboard drawing: focus the paper with Tab. Arrow keys move the pen; hold Shift for larger steps. Space starts or finishes a stroke. Escape discards the unfinished stroke. A cross marks your pen.

Rotations copy around the centre. Mirror reflects across both axes. Tile repeats in a 3 × 3 grid. Change the repeat or palette after drawing; your original strokes stay editable. Undo remembers 20 changes in this session.
